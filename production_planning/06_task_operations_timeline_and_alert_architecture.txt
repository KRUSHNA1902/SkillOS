--- NEW & EXCLUSIVE IN THIS VERSION (06) ---
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Centralized Task Management Engine (TaskManagerProvider & useTaskManager):
   - Backed by persistent browser storage (`localStorage`) with automated seed data fallback.
   - Cross-workspace synchronization between Dashboard and dedicated Tasks workspace.
   - Unified live metrics (Total, In Progress, Completed, Pending, Blocked, Urgent, Overdue, Due Today, Completion Rate).

2. Rich Task Creation & Editing (TaskModal):
   - Fast inline task addition for quick capture.
   - Comprehensive task dialog supporting:
     * Title with validation
     * Category tagging (Frontend UI, Backend, Auth Pipeline, Security, AI Engine, DevOps, etc.)
     * 4-Level Priority System (Urgent, High, Medium, Low) with visual chromatic badges
     * ISO Target Deadline selector with dynamic +2h, Tonight, +24h, and +3d presets
     * Multi-phase Timeline Stages (Planning, In Progress, Review, Completed, Backlog)
     * Technical Notes & Scratchpad (documentation, acceptance criteria, checklists)
     * Toggleable Deadline Alert Reminders

3. Task Pruning & Deletion Operations ("Deleting Old"):
   - Individual task deletion with interactive trash trigger.
   - 1-Click "Clean Old Tasks" modal dialog: safely removes completed sprint tasks while preserving active and pending tasks.

4. Chronological Delivery Timeline (TaskTimelineView):
   - Dedicated "Timeline View" tab alongside standard "List View".
   - Vertical milestone line with pulsing progress nodes grouped into:
     * Overdue Milestones (high priority attention)
     * Today's Delivery Window
     * Tomorrow & Next 48 Hours
     * Sprint Target Milestones (later this week)
     * Backlog & Long-Term Roadmap
     * Completed Milestone Archive

5. Real-Time Deadline & Schedule Alert System (TaskAlertsBanner):
   - Prominent notification alert bar at the top of the Tasks workspace and Dashboard.
   - Automated detection of:
     * Overdue tasks with elapsed duration badges
     * Due today tasks with deadline countdowns
     * Custom deadline alert reminders
   - 1-Click Quick Actions directly from alerts: "Done" (instant completion), "+1 Day" (deadline extension), and "Dismiss".

6. Priority & Notes Interaction:
   - 1-Click priority cycling directly on task cards (Urgent -> High -> Medium -> Low).
   - Expandable and inline markdown/code note drawers on task cards.
